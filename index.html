01 — About

From tackling opponents to tackling algorithms.

I'm a former professional athlete turned software engineer. I love competitive, fast-paced environments, whether that's on the field or in a codebase, and football taught me to adapt, solve problems and perform under pressure.

I played defensive tackle at Cal Poly, where I was a two-time captain and FCS All-American, then spent time with the San Francisco 49ers. After football I completed App Academy's full-stack program, contributed to open-source civic tech with SF Civic Tech, and joined Saleo. When Saleo was acquired by Consensus, I came along, and today I'm a software engineer at Consensus working across TypeScript, React, Node.js, AWS and Terraform.

Outside of code, I'm a husband and dad, I still get to the gym whenever I can, and I'm a lifelong fan of the 49ers, Warriors and Giants.
