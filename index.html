Football taught me to read the field. Engineering is the same game.

I'm a former professional athlete turned software engineer. I love competitive, fast-paced environments, on the field or in a codebase.

I played defensive tackle at Cal Poly, where I was a two-time captain and FCS All-American, then spent time with the San Francisco 49ers. After football I completed App Academy's full-stack program, contributed to open-source civic tech with SF Civic Tech, and joined Saleo. When Consensus acquired Saleo, I came along. Today I work across TypeScript, React, Node.js, AWS and Terraform.

Outside of code, I'm a husband and dad, I still get to the gym whenever I can, and I'm a lifelong fan of the 49ers, Warriors and Giants.
